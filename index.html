01 The studio

From Sri Ganganagar to the banks of the Ganga.

8count Dance Club started in Sri Ganganagar, Rajasthan as a home for dancers of every age, from first-timers to brides, grooms and competition kids. Now we're bringing that energy to Tapovan, Rishikesh, with a new studio at RR Stay & Cafe.

Every class is built on the count of eight: learn the beat, own the move, then let go and enjoy it.
